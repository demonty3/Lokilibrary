# Cell density — can the world afford a finer lattice?

**2026-08-17.** Opened from the mural-weave verdict (same day): the 44×10
mosaic was "clearly the best" purely on cell count, and Harry asked the
foundational form of the question — "a more foundational cell rework, like
seeing if we can get better results on fidelity with this art technique."

## 1 · The standing ground

The desk renders Cozette 6×13 at `WORLD_SCALE 2` — 12×26-px cells, a 53×20
grid in a 640×520 window. Every judged eyeball to date (murals, marginalia,
land polish, liveliness, the stitch packs) was judged at this scale. The
moat (CLAUDE.md): stylised pixel art that *reads as terminal*, legible as a
wallpaper at glance distance; sub-character animation depends on cells
being big enough for sub-cell motion to be visible. The reference
cross-stitch piece gets its fidelity from a finer lattice: material
motif-fields at small cell size, detail from density and rhythm.

## 2 · The question

Rendered at `WORLD_SCALE 1` — 6×13-px cells, a 106×40 grid in the same
window, 4× the cells — does the same wing read as a **richer place** (the
reference's density) while staying legible as a wallpaper, or does it
collapse into texture?

## 3 · Kill conditions — frozen 2026-08-17, before the side-by-side was generated or viewed

> **K1 (glance value, the moat):** at wallpaper distance, if the scale-1
> world's beings and site labels stop being findable/readable — if you
> have to lean in to find the life — fidelity has beaten the product and
> the global scale stays 2. Fidelity then routes to MIXED REGISTERS only
> (fine-lattice surfaces inside the coarse world: the mural rect, far
> tapestry layers), not a world rework.

> **K2 (the letter-noise failure at world scale):** if the finer grid
> reads as noise or fabric-in-general rather than as a *more detailed
> place* — the "unreadable ae's" finding recurring globally — same
> routing as K1.

Pass is the reference's read: a denser, richer world that still glances
well. A pass does NOT ship anything: it opens an engine question (scale as
a dial, every salience bar re-measured at the new denominator, sub-cell
animation re-dialled) that gets its own spec and its own bars.

## 4 · The probe

`2026-08-17-cell-density.html`: the same wing (d0), composed by the real
composer at both geometries — 53×20 rendered at 26px (today's desk,
byte-honest) beside 106×40 rendered at 13px (the scale-1 candidate; the
composer given the extra cells genuinely composes more world: more sites,
wider relief, denser sky). Same window pixel size, same pack (phosphor),
beings and masthead drawn in both. Judged at wallpaper distance. Verdict
recorded below, dated.
